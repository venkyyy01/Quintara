claims common-law rights in the name and logo. No federal, Canadian, or other

Use the **™** symbol with QUINTARA. Do not use **®** unless and until a
registration issues in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Adverse QUINTARA registration

A live US trademark registration for **QUINTARA** already exists in Class 9:
Registration No. 2,937,944, filed 31 December 2003, registered 5 April 2005,
renewed 17 August 2025, owned by **Brilliant Systems LLC** for computer software
for storing email on a server computer. This policy does not claim priority over
that registration, and the Project Owner does not own it. A browser game may be
treated as software for trademark purposes, so this is a real clearance risk.
Do not assume that common-law use of QUINTARA is safe for a commercial launch;
obtain a written clearance opinion before investing in the brand.
